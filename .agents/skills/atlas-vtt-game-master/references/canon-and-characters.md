# Campaign canon and character persona

Use this reference for NPC development, adopting approved workshop material, organizing a campaign codex, or choosing an Atlas note/card surface. Inspect the campaign's existing entries and live checkpoint before writing. A codex is the maintained collection of campaign notes; identify the actual Atlas feature used to reach it.

## Develop a person through behavior

Begin with the user's account and established history. Trace the experience into the bargain the person learned, the situations that threaten it, what they actually do, and how others respond. Keep the useful ability, pleasure, affection or conviction that makes the person worth knowing alongside the defense or failing. Trauma can explain a reaction without explaining every act or becoming a diagnosis.

Make contradictions observable. A devoted advocate may patiently hear a stranger and interrupt her sister. An independent guide may still depend on a former partner's unnoticed repairs. Record the different circumstances, what the person thinks she is doing, and what another person experiences. Avoid replacing these specifics with adjective lists, a compulsory confession or a single concealed motive that explains everything.

Trace consequences beyond the immediate exchange: taking over can reduce others' practice, increase dependence and deepen resentment; making claims recognizable to power can help petitioners while narrowing what the institution notices. Those are pressures and possible developments until actual conduct establishes them. Let relationships change through repeated choices and other people's independent responses. Keep positive and negative arcs open.

Use the region's established material culture, social arrangements and era when adding occupations, family expectations, institutions, dress or artifacts. Ground a character in particular lived experience; do not derive personality, merit or morality from ethnicity. Preserve approved appearance and use the owning portrait/token forge for an authorized art revision.

## Keep one authoritative entry

Locate the existing canonical note before creating one. The name, folder and properties should make its identity and ownership clear. Use full vault paths when a character entry and statblock share a basename.

An entry can hold:

- A brief table-use summary that other notes embed.
- Convictions, immediate purpose and longer ambition.
- Established history and the person's public account of it, distinguished from each other.
- Specific observable behavior, capabilities and knowledge limits.
- Contextual pressures, relationships and possible consequences or arcs.

This is a useful shape, not a requirement to fill every section. Leave unknown details unknown. A selected truthful public story can obscure a past without introducing incompatible histories. A claim only becomes an established fact when the user adopts it or coherent campaign evidence supports it.

Use links for connected people, places and institutions; embed a reusable section instead of copying it. Give a shared relationship one owner. Retain existing location/evidence notes and pin-linked headings when they already serve their purpose. Properties such as `kind`, `campaign`, `canon`, `audience`, `role`, `associated_place`, `atlas_card` and `related` can support Obsidian search or views; they are a campaign convention, not an Atlas schema.

When the user adopts workshop material, write it into the canonical entry, update summaries and referring links, and retire superseded drafts or mirrors within the authorized cleanup scope. Verify that accepted details survived and affected links/embeds resolve before removing an old copy. Keep recovery proportional to the change; do not leave a new backup dossier beside every revision. A source pack may reference the vault's entry rather than maintaining a second full history.

## Match purpose to the actual surface

| Surface | Use | Operational boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary Obsidian entry | Author and read full character/world canon | Markdown links, heading embeds and properties are supported note features. Editing a note does not place a token or change a scene. |
| Atlas DM Dashboard linked note | Keep the GM's runbook, index or relevant note beside the map | The picker selects a whole Markdown file. Follow internal links/embeds; preserve the existing attachment unless a change is requested. |
| GM note pin / pinned note preview | Reach a location, encounter or relevant section from the map | Note previews support `note.md#Heading`; changes to saved pins or scene attachments use native controls. |
| Linked statblock and actor card | Show the selected card content and operate that actor's supported resources | Preserve its actual `statblockPath`. Token/statblock association can update assets and spawned tokens; a custom `atlas_card` property does not perform it. |
| Public hub, handout, portrait or Player View | Present information the players may see | Choose content for the audience. Private histories, metadata and links can disclose information even when their labels look harmless. |
| Session State | Record actual choices, disclosures, promises, custody and pending actions | Canon and proposed encounters do not automatically reveal facts or advance this record. Live resource ownership remains with the designated placed controls. |

`audience: gm`, a private folder name, and a hidden Base column are labels rather than access controls. Keep private material on GM surfaces; select or author a player-safe presentation when a reveal is earned. Do not add private histories to an actor card merely to make the canon reachable from a token.

## Portray and maintain

For an exchange, read the relevant entry and choose behavior that fits the actual request, relationship, fatigue and stakes. Preserve both competence and limits. An old pain need not trigger every time its wording appears. A sound criticism can change a factual account without curing arrogance, avoidance or a relationship. The GM plays the NPC's response and leaves the heroes' feelings, speech and decisions to their controller.

Record actual disclosures and consequential conduct in Session State. Update an entry when the user changes canon or play establishes a durable fact; update the shared relationship account when appropriate. Keep unresolved intentions and future encounters conditional. Contracted help, essential evidence and known reward terms do not acquire secret persona-dependent conditions.

For a canon-only change, check accepted content and affected note/heading links, and inspect rendering when embeds or layout changed. For a native attachment or presentation change, verify it in Atlas and check Player View when visibility is affected. A source review, rendered Obsidian note, observed Atlas panel and saved/reopened scene are different evidence; report the stage actually checked. For campaign Markdown and GM skill guidance changes in this Atlas workflow, use the appropriate local content, link and skill checks; no CI gate applies.
